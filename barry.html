Supreme Campus Legend 🌟

Barry the Squirrel

Chief Security Companion & Supreme Biscuit Connoisseur

The Tale of Barry & The Security Guard

Long before the other squirrels claimed their little territories in the hall garden, there was Barry—the undisputed king of Headington Hill. Barry did not scamper away into the leafy treetops when people walked past. Instead, he waddled proudly down to the security lodge every single morning to report for duty!

The campus security guard at the Headington Hill site formed a legendary bond of trust with Barry, gently taming him with secret pocket treats, wholesome seeds, and warm digestive crumbs. Soon, Barry became the official honorary guardian of the gates. Students, lecturers, and university administrators alike stopped in their tracks just to watch Barry sit upright on his chubby hind legs, politely holding a walnut in both paws like an English gentleman attending high tea.

The Anatomy of Pure Chonky Cuteness

Barry is famous for his adorably plump belly, his delightfully pudgy cheeks that could store half a dozen hazelnuts at once, and a coat so thick and plush he looked like a toasted marshmallow with a tail. Whenever the autumn wind rustled through the oak trees, Barry didn't shiver—his magnificent roundness kept him warm all season long. His paws were tiny and polite, his eyes were bright as shiny black buttons, and when he ran across the lawn, it wasn't a sprint; it was a rhythmic, cheerful waddle that brought joy to everyone having a stressful exam week.

Have You Seen Barry Anywhere?

Headington Hill Campus Mystery & Grand Adventure Notice

Last Known Location: Headington Hill Security Post

He's been gone for quite a while now...

One crisp autumn day after a wonderful feast of acorns and biscuits at the guard lodge, Barry gave a twitch of his bushy tail, waddled past the barrier gate, and headed into the great canopy beyond. Days turned into weeks, and weeks turned into seasons—and our beloved chubby companion hasn't checked in with security!
